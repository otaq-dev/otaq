## Findings
1. ✅ **Decided (0047 refined): they sort right after the orange cards.** Pinned → orange → Interrupted / Start interrupted /
   Restack conflict / Parent closed → working → Paused. On the main board, SHOP-25 must be visible right below SHOP-12.
   Originally: **Sort order for "needs you, but not orange" tickets is undefined, and it hides one on the board.** On the main
   board, **SHOP-25 (Interrupted) is folded into "4 more" at the bottom of Dev**, below the working cards. The decisions
   order Dev as pinned → orange → working → Paused (0042, 0047), but don't place Interrupted, Restack conflict, Parent
   closed or Start interrupted, which are inbox group 2. Recommendation: they sort **right after the orange cards**, matching
   the inbox order. **This needs the user's OK.**
2. **Tab labels in the narrow tab bars** (frames 06, 10 and 13; about 36 labels) are still flagged as clipped by the layout
   check. The icon-only-tabs fix from update C may not be applied everywhere. Verify, and fix where it isn't.
3. **The "⇧⌘C" shortcut chip** is clipped in "03 · Board & columns" (fully) and "01 · Components".
4. **Card variant "Depends on an epic"** truncates its text: "waiting on epic SHOP-40 (2 / 3 do…".
5. *(Optional)* **Epic children show twice in Backlog:** SHOP-40.3 is listed inside the SHOP-40 epic card and also as its own
   card. That's fine, since the standalone card is the draggable one, but consider dimming the epic's list rows when the child is
   visible in the same column.
