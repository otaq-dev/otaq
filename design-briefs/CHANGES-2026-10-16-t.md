# Design update T: recorded decisions, lead notes and the same-file flag (2026-10-16)

Decision **0084** (new) and the later refinements of **0083** add things update S didn't draw:
- **Recorded decisions (0084).** When an agent meets a product question that the code and documents don't settle, it
  asks only if it can't go on without the answer. Otherwise it builds one option and records the choice on its ticket.
  Every recorded decision is listed in the ticket's review and in its epic's review. Approve keeps them all; Change…
  sends the ticket back, or adds a fix child in an epic's review.
- **The lead's mark (0083).** On each merge the lead reads the child's recorded decisions, passes them on to children
  that haven't started, and marks a decision that a written source contradicts. It never overrules one.
- **Unread notes (0083).** A lead's note to a working child waits as unread; the agent reads it before each commit
  (`otaq notes`) and in `otaq prime`; `otaq review submit` is refused while one is unread.
- **Same file (0083).** Otaq flags two running children that changed the same file, on both cards, without waking the
  lead; the lead can make a never-started child wait.

It also carries the three fixes from the audit of update S. Apply after update S. Affected briefs: 03, 04, 05, 06, 07.
